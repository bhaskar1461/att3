## 📱 Section 1: 1-Time Setup (Choose Your Phone)

### Option A: Android (Google Chrome)
1. Open **Google Chrome** and navigate to: `https://ather-os.de5.net`
2. Tap the **three-dot menu (⋮)** in the top right corner.
3. Tap **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
4. Confirm by tapping **"Install"**. The SNIST portal icon will appear on your phone home screen.

### Option B: iPhone / iPad (Apple Safari)
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **iPhone Requirement**: Apple requires Web Apps to be installed using **Safari**. If opened in Chrome or Brave, copy the link and open it in Safari.

1. Open **Safari** and navigate to: `https://ather-os.de5.net`
2. Tap the **Share button** (square with an upward arrow) at the bottom center.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **"Add"** in the top right. Launch the app by tapping the **SNIST ERP** icon on your home screen.

## 🔐 Section 2: First Login & Automatic Device Binding
1. Tap the **SNIST ERP** icon on your **Home Screen** (not inside a browser tab).
2. Enter your **Roll Number / SAP ID** and your **Portal Password**.
3. Upon first successful login, **your physical phone is permanently paired** with your student account.
4. From now on, your attendance QR scans will only be accepted from this approved device.

---

## 🔄 Section 3: Lost, Damaged, or Replaced Phone? (Self-Service Recovery)
If you switch to a new phone, follow this 30-second self-service reset:
1. Open the portal on your **new phone**.
2. On the login screen, click **"Lost or replaced phone? Reset device"**.
3. Enter your **Roll Number** and **Password** ➔ Tap **"Send Verification Code"**.
4. Check your **official college email** for the 6-digit one-time code.
5. Enter the code and tap **"Authorize This Phone & Login"**.
*Note: Students can perform up to 5 self-service device resets per semester. Beyond 5, please contact your faculty mentor.*

## 🛠️ Section 4: Troubleshooting FAQ

| Problem | Cause | Exact Solution |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **"Install App to Scan" modal appears** | You opened the portal in a browser tab instead of the home screen app. | Close your browser. Find the **SNIST ERP** app icon on your home screen and open it. |
| **"Account registered to a different device"** | Logging in from an unapproved phone or browser. | Click **"Reset Device Binding via Email OTP"** on the login screen to authorize your phone. |
| **"Session Token Expired" during scan** | Rotating classroom QR refreshed. | Point camera back at the projector screen. It will scan the fresh QR within 1 second. |
| **No camera prompt appears** | Camera permissions blocked in OS settings. | Open phone Settings ➔ App Permissions ➔ Allow Camera access for your browser/PWA. |
| **Phone battery died or camera broken** | Physical hardware issue. | Inform your faculty member. They can mark you present directly on their class roster. |
