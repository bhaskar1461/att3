SNIST Academic Attendance Portal

1-Time Mobile PWA Installation & Attendance Setup • Academic Year 2026

Official Guide
Important Requirement: To prevent attendance fraud, the attendance camera scanner activates only inside the installed Home Screen app. Do not scan from inside browser tabs.

📱 Step 1: Install App on Your Phone (Takes 10 Seconds)

Android (Google Chrome)
  1. Open Chrome and go to: https://ather-os.de5.net
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in top right.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm install. Open SNIST ERP from your home screen.
[ SCREENSHOT: Chrome ⋮ Menu > Install app ]
iPhone / iPad (Apple Safari)
  1. Open Safari and go to: https://ather-os.de5.net
  2. Tap the Share button (square with up arrow at bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add". Open SNIST ERP from your home screen.
[ SCREENSHOT: Safari Share > Add to Home Screen ]

🔐 Step 2: First Login & Automatic Device Binding

  1. Launch the app by tapping the SNIST ERP icon on your home screen.
  2. Log in with your Roll Number / SAP ID and Portal Password.
  3. Upon successful login, your phone is automatically registered to your student account.
  4. Future scans will only be accepted from this physical device.

🔄 Step 3: Changed or Replaced Your Phone? (Self-Service Reset)

If you replace your phone or get locked out with "Account registered to a different device":

  1. On the login page of your new phone, click "Lost or replaced phone? Reset device".
  2. Enter your Roll Number and Password > Click "Send Verification Code".
  3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your official college email and tap "Authorize This Phone".
  4. Your new phone is immediately bound. (Limit: 5 self-resets per semester).

🛠️ Quick Troubleshooting FAQ

Symptom Action to Resolve
"Install App to Scan" prompt appears You opened the portal in a browser tab. Close your browser and launch the app from the SNIST ERP icon on your home screen.
"Registered to a different device" Click the "Reset Device Binding via Email OTP" link on the login screen to authorize your current phone.
"Session Token Expired" The classroom projector rotates QR codes every 10 seconds. Keep camera pointed at the projector screen.
Phone broken / dead battery Inform your class teacher. Faculty can mark your attendance manually on their class roster.
